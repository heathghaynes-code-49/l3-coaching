The weight

When was the last time someone asked how you're really doing?

Not how's business. How are you? In the business. At home. In your faith. It's all connected, it's heavy, and most owners carry it alone, in rooms where everyone looks like they've got it together.

»

The business is the hard part.

You started this thing, and it's harder than anyone told you. Most of what you're figuring out, you're figuring out alone.

»

Home is the hard part.

The business is working, but the people at home are getting what's left of you rather than the best of you. You already know it.

»

Faith and work run on separate tracks.

You take Jesus seriously and you take your business seriously. You're just not sure anyone else is trying to bring them together.

You can only lead people as far as you've gone yourself.

Every owner carries two kinds of weakness: gaps you can name, and blind spots you can't see until someone who loves you says so out loud.

Most owners swing between holding on too tight and quietly checking out. The goal is to free the people around you to do their best work. That starts with you.

What is Iron Forums?

Iron Forums is a community for Christian business owners who want to grow—but know they can’t do it alone. We exist to help leaders Connect relationally, Sharpen each other spiritually and professionally, and Grow in every area that matters: faith, business, and family.

We meet for a half-day Forum once a month where real growth happens—through shared wisdom, accountability, and deep encouragement from like-minded peers. If you're serious about growing as a leader and a follower of Christ, this is the space for you. Learn more about Iron Forums →

Who is it for?

Iron Forums is for owners, entrepreneurs and solo-prenuers who are humble, hungry, and led by the Holy Spirit—or desire to be. If you're willing to be sharpened, want to grow with others, and are ready to lead your company from a place of Christ-centered clarity, you're in the right place.

We find our sweet spot in working with small business owners that have 2-50 employees. This helps keep the topics relevant to all and the snapshot groups valuable. We are industry agnostic and because this is not a networking group, we have no industry cap.

How is Iron Forums different than C12, Convene and other groups?

We’re thankful for and supportive of groups like C12, Convene, and Faith Driven Entrepreneur. Each of us serves the Kingdom in complementary ways. Iron Forums is specifically designed for owners of businesses under $20M in revenue—leaders who are often in the trenches, wearing many hats, and seeking both community and practical growth.

Is this more like a business mastermind or a Bible study?

It’s more than both. Iron Forums combines faith + business + community in a way that transforms lives. It’s not just information or networking—it’s a guided process that helps you identify what God is doing in your life and leadership, and equips you to take action.

How do you define "small business"?

We define a small business as a company with 2 to 50 employees. That's not a hard-and-fast rule, and we look at each owner case by case. Why a company is small doesn't matter. Maybe it's brand new, maybe it's small by design, maybe it's something else entirely. If you carry the weight of the company, we'd love to talk about whether this community fits.
