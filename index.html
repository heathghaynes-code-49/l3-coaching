Where is your leadership stuck?

The question

"Who are you becoming as a leader, and is your calendar telling the same story?"

Tap to see the principle & solution
01

The principle

Your calendar is your character in calendar form. Change the blocks, and you change the leader.

The practical move

Audit one week. Color-code every hour as Building People, Building Strategy, Building Yourself, or Putting Out Fires. The ratio is your real leadership, not your job description.

02

The question

"What conversation have you been avoiding, and what is it costing the person you're not having it with?"

Tap to see the principle & solution
02

The principle

Silence is not kindness. The conversation you owe them is the one that sets them free.

The practical move

Name the one person. Write the one sentence you've been swallowing. Schedule it inside 7 days. Liberators say the hard thing early; Dominators say it late and loud; Abdicators never say it at all.

03

The question

"If your team described your leadership honestly, what would they say, and what would need to change?"

Tap to see the principle & solution
03

The principle

You don't get to define your leadership, your team does. Lead like they're already telling the truth about you.

The practical move

Ask three trusted people one question: "What's it like on the other side of me?" Don't defend the answers. Adjust one behavior this month. That single loop is how reputation becomes reality.

04

The question

"If we asked every person on your team what 'winning' looks like this quarter, would we get the same answer four times, or four different ones?"

Tap to see the principle & solution
04

The principle

Clarity is a multiplier. The cost of unclear targets isn't slower work, it's smart people pulling in opposite directions, all looking like they're failing.

The practical move

Write the one-sentence "win" for this quarter. Test it with three people on the team this week. If you get three different versions back, you don't have a team problem, you have a clarity problem. Fix that first.

If any of these landed, the Diagnostic will tell you where to start.
