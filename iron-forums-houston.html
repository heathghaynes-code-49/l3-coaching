The weight

When was the last time someone asked how you're really doing?

Not how's business. How are you? In the business. At home. In your faith. It's all connected, it's heavy, and most owners carry it alone, in rooms where everyone looks like they've got it together.

»

The business is the hard part.

You started this thing, and it's harder than anyone told you. Most of what you're figuring out, you're figuring out alone.

»

Home is the hard part.

The business is working, but the people at home are getting what's left of you rather than the best of you. You already know it.

»

Faith and work run on separate tracks.

You take Jesus seriously and you take your business seriously. You're just not sure anyone else is trying to bring them together.

You can only lead people as far as you've gone yourself.

Every owner carries two kinds of weakness: gaps you can name, and blind spots you can't see until someone who loves you says so out loud.

Most owners swing between holding on too tight and quietly checking out. The goal is to free the people around you to do their best work. That starts with you.

What it is

A monthly table for owners who get what you carry.

An Iron Forum is where kingdom-minded small business owners do the honest work of growing their faith, their family, and their business, together, with people who won't let them coast.

Your Houston Forum Leader

Meet Heath Haynes.

Heath has spent thirty years in full-time ministry, starting at 19. He's been a worship pastor, an associate pastor in a multi-site church of over 50,000, and since 2013, a church planter. He leads The Bridge HTX, a network of home churches that started in Montrose, in one of the most diverse cities in America.

He coaches church planters, and since 2023 he entered into coaching owners and leaders in the marketplace, so he's carried a business of his own too. His life's work is making leaders worth following.

He's not the expert at the front of the room. He's the one saving you a seat at the table. An Atlanta native, Heath lives in Houston with his wife of 20 years, Amber, and their two teenagers, and he's never far from a good coffee shop.

Questions

Not ready to register? Ask away.

Who is it for?

Iron Forums is built for small business owners, men and women who carry the weight of a company and want their faith, their family, and their business to grow together. If you're the one everything runs through, and you don't have a table of peers who understand that, this is for you.

What does the Discovery Lunch cost?

Nothing. Lunch is on us. Seats are limited to 40, and registering helps us order the right amount of food.

Where do we meet, and what about parking?

1801 Main St., 11th Floor, Houston, TX 77002. We'll send parking instructions with your registration confirmation.

I can't make November 17. What now?

Use the form and check the box to be notified. We'll email you when registration opens for the Pilot Forum on December 10 and the January 14 kickoff.
