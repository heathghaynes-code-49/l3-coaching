- *"If I don't do it, it won't get done."*
- *"It's just easier if I handle it myself."*
- *"They're not ready to take ownership yet."*

Sound familiar?

If you're a high-capacity leader, these phrases might echo in your head or overtake your calendar more than you'd like to admit. They're the refrain of a common but costly leadership trap:

{% pullQuote "OVER-FUNCTIONING." %}

It looks like dedication. It feels like responsibility. But it often leads to exhaustion, frustration, and — ironically — a disempowered team.

Here is why this matters. If you want to lead at your full potential and enable your team to do the same, you'll need to stop doing too much and start leading with intention.

## What Is Over-functioning?

Over-functioning is when a leader consistently takes on more responsibility than they should. It's when you:

- Solve the problem before others have a chance
- Carry your team's emotional load and your own
- Constantly step in, follow up, rescue, and cover for others

It's not because you're weak. It's because you care. You're committed. You want things to go well. But over-functioning, if left unchecked, becomes the bottleneck to your growth — and your team's.

Over-functioning leaders often wear invisible capes, but here's the truth:

{% pullQuote "You weren't meant to be the hero of your team. You're meant to be the guide — the \"heromaker.\"" %}

## What It Costs You (and Them)

When you over-function:

- You become overwhelmed and resentful
- Your team becomes passive and dependent
- Initiative disappears
- Creativity stalls
- Ownership declines

What feels like help becomes control. What started as a desire to lead well results in a culture of low trust and low empowerment.

It's unsustainable for you. And it's unproductive for them.

At L3 Coaching, as part of the GiANT Worldwide family, we've worked with hundreds of leaders stuck in this exact cycle — and the breakthrough almost always comes from the same place:

{% pullQuote "A SHIFT IN MINDSET." %}

That's why we equip our clients with the **Leader Mindset Questions** — a simple tool that unlocks clarity and helps leaders move from over-functioning to intentional development.

In [a previous post](/blog/wheres-my-magic-bullet.html), we explained a foundational concept to being a Liberating Leader called the Support/Challenge Matrix. If you haven't read that yet, I encourage you to take a few minutes to go read that post as well. For now, the Leader Mindset Questions:

These three Leader Mindset Questions reframe your thinking and re-center your role as a leader:

1. What specific support and challenge do they need from me?
   - *(Not what's easiest for me to give, but what they actually need to grow.)*
2. What is the tendency or pattern most undermining their influence?
   - *(Name the thing that's holding them back — so you can coach them through it.)*
3. How do I help them get to the next level?
   - *(Shift from doing to developing. That's where true leadership lives.)*

## What's the Alternative to Over-functioning?

The alternative isn't under-functioning.

It's becoming a **Liberating Leader** — someone who leads themself well and brings calibrated support and challenge to others with intentionality.

And that begins not with a new strategy, but a new mindset. You don't need to carry the whole weight. You need to equip others to carry theirs.

## Try This Today

Here's a 3-step exercise to move from over-functioning to leading:

1. **Reflect**
   - Where are you rescuing someone instead of developing them?
2. **Apply the Leader Mindset Questions**
   - Pause and ask:
     - What support and challenge do they really need?
     - What tendency is limiting their impact?
     - What's the next developmental step?
3. **Act**
   - This week, have one intentional conversation that shifts your posture from doing *for* to calling *up*.
